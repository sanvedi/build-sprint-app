## Final 1: Explain the library change

Write a notice that helps students plan a library visit and return books during a closure. Facts: closed Monday; reopens Tuesday at 9 AM; outside book-return box remains available during the closure. Exactly two bullet points, at most 60 words, in plain words. No invented fees, closure reason or arrangements.

Starting prompt: Write about the library.

A useful request specifies a notice and the required format, and includes or clearly refers to the supplied facts. It needs no role formula or exact phrasing. A sound judgment checks the actual answer's facts, return-box availability, format, length and invented arrangements. A student may correctly reject a flawed answer despite a sensible request. A lucky answer cannot make an incomplete request pass.

## Alternate: Explain the study group

Give first-year students the details needed to join an optional study group. Facts: Thursday, 4 PM, Room 108, 45 minutes; optional, first-year students, bring a notebook. Exactly two sentences, at most 60 words, friendly tone. No invented fee, registration or extra materials.

Starting prompt: Tell students about studying together.

A sound request and judgment cover the task, desired answer and actual facts. Judge this generated answer, not a reference or previous final's answer. Neither independent final requires a correction explanation.

## Progress and practice return

Both practices must finish, with or without points, before opening a final. Convex marks a variant seen before displaying its task. Reopening resumes the same unassessed variant. One valid assessment completes its independent attempt; service failure consumes none. A justified pass earns the Beginner badge, without another practice point.

A prompt gap returns to a separate supported round of practice 1; a judgment gap returns to practice 2. Those rounds retain submitted history, permit initial plus two retries, and earn no additional independent points. Completing the round with or without help permits the unseen alternate. After both variants fail, further practice remains available but no fresh final or level unlock is offered. Rechecking the latest final can correct its decision without adding an attempt, including revoking a mistaken badge.

Public final opening and new awards require PROMPT_GAME_FINALS_ENABLED=true in Convex. It is enabled only in development for this implementation; production remains unchanged. Do not enable public automatic awards until material review and feedback-gate evidence are recorded.
