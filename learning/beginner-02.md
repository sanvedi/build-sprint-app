# Beginner practice 2: make the desired answer clear

Status: prepared synthetic challenge, awaiting teacher review before release. Version: beginner-02-v1. The screen and Convex use the same material in shared/practiceTasks.mjs.

## Task and starting prompt

Turn submission details into instructions a student can follow without guessing. Submit one PDF through the course portal by Tuesday at 5 PM; name the file student-ID_report.pdf. The answer must use plain words, exactly three numbered steps and at most 70 words. Do not invent a link, fee or extra requirement.

Starting prompt: Tell students how to submit their work.

Prepared original answer: Finish your work and send it to your teacher soon. Visit the submission website and pay the processing fee if needed.

That example omits the actual deadline, portal and filename, invents a possible fee, and does not provide the requested three steps. It is illustrative material, not a recorded AI response.

## Assessment

The request must make the desired instructions and output format clear. The task facts are available to the answering model; referring clearly to those supplied facts is allowed. Do not require a role instruction, exact phrasing or a longer prompt for its own sake.

Judge the actual generated answer against all four requirements. A lucky correct answer cannot rescue an unclear request. A sensible request can earn its point even if the answer is poor, provided the student identifies the material flaws accurately.

For prompt gaps, edit the supplied prompt, explain the relevant change, generate a replacement answer and judge that new answer. For judgment gaps, keep the existing prompt and answer and correct the judgment with an explanation. The initial attempt plus two retries belong only to this practice. A successful initial attempt or justified correction earns at most one point.

## Worked example after both retries

Prompt: Write submission instructions in plain words, exactly three numbered steps and at most 70 words. Students must submit one PDF through the course portal by Tuesday at 5 PM. The filename must be student-ID_report.pdf. Do not invent a link, fee or extra requirement.

Illustrative answer:

1. Save your work as one PDF.
2. Name the file student-ID_report.pdf, using your student ID.
3. Submit the PDF through the course portal by Tuesday at 5 PM.

The request specifies the task, format, facts and limits. The answer includes the supplied details in three short steps. A student must still inspect any actual generated answer.

Teacher review must check the requirements, both correction paths and what counts as sound judgment before public automatic awards. No such review or measured learning result is claimed here.
