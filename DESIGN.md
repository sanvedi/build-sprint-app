# Prompting Game: interface brief

Product truth: PRODUCT.md. Build order: IDEA_SCOPE.md. These are layout intentions, not a completed or browser-verified game design.

## Direction

A focused practice space for students: the task, edit and resulting answer carry the screen. Preserve the simple readable setup page until the first verified game flow is ready. Choose the full visual identity during that build; no previous reminder-inbox design governs it.

## Challenge layout

Show the task and essential requirements first, then a labelled editable prompt. Keep the original answer available while reading the generated one: side by side on wide screens, stacked with clear labels on phones. Collect the student's answer judgment before revealing feedback. Use explicit buttons such as Generate answer, Check my judgment and Try my correction.

Feedback identifies a specific gap, asks for a correction and one or two sentences explaining it, then shows whether the gap was resolved. If only judgment needs changing, reuse the existing answer. Show points only after confirmed assessment and saving. An early correct attempt also earns its point; replays do not.

## Game states

Display level and practice/final status, attempts remaining and earned points without a leaderboard, streak or timer. Successful independent finals earn a badge and unlock the next level. Pro completes the game. Keep unsupported example-viewing distinct from independent success.

Finals hide hints and worked examples until submission. After failure offer practice and an unseen reviewed variant. If none remains, explain why progression is locked rather than pretend a repeated task is fresh.

## Access and failure

Start without a required account; offer saving through sign-in after first value. Preserve the guest attempt. Explain device-only saving versus account backup. Show saving, saved and failed states truthfully. Failed generation preserves the edit and does not use an attempt. Unavailable assessment keeps the result pending, with retry; exhausted usage preserves work and explains when to return.

## Readability

Use labelled fields, visible keyboard focus, errors linked to fields and text that communicates status without color alone. Support narrow phones and enlarged text, comfortable tap targets and reduced motion. Keep explanations brief; do not reward grammar, length or mandatory role formulas.

The local setup page remains an honest placeholder: no disabled game-like controls, fabricated points or claims of a working AI game.
